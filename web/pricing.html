Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up and running PrintAway at your stationery shop.

Does PrintAway work if internet drops?

Yes! Local Counter Admin works 100% offline at http://127.0.0.1:8080/admin. Any walk-in customer connected to your shop WiFi can print without internet. When internet is live, Cloudflare tunnel connects students anywhere on campus.

Can I still collect cash at the counter?

Absolutely. Customers can tap "Pay at Counter" on their phone. The order shows in your admin queue highlighted in yellow. You collect cash, click Approve & Print, and the hardware begins printing immediately.

How does B&W vs Color multi-printer auto-routing work?

If you have a dedicated high-speed B&W machine (like a Xerox or Canon heavy-duty runner) and a Color printer, you simply select them in your Live Orders hardware routing bar. B&W jobs automatically go to your B&W machine, and color jobs go to your color printer with zero manual intervention!
